How to start a raised-bed vegetable garden

Published March 14, 2016. A raised bed warms earlier in spring, drains better, and keeps your soil loose. This guide covers siting, materials, soil mix, and a first planting plan.

Choosing a spot

Pick a level area with at least six hours of direct sun and easy access to water.
